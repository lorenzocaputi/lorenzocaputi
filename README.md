I'm an MS student in Statistics at Stanford and a graduate researcher at MIT with Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas. Before this, I studied Computer Science and Economics at Bocconi and wrote my thesis at Harvard with Prof. Tomer Ullman. I also co-founded [BAINSA](https://bainsa.ai), Italy's largest student-run AI research lab.

My research is on building intelligent systems that create new scientific knowledge, autonomously and safely. In particular:
- how to teach a language model "research taste" using signal from its own exploration;
- how to use test-time compute to explore the space of text-based solutions to open scientific problems, for example through evolutionary search or test-time learning;
- how to use these methods reliably on high-impact open problems in science and industrial R&D.

#### Projects

- [**Probabilistic matrix factorization**](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/probabilistic-matrix-factorization): seven ways to fill in a matrix with missing entries, compared on simulated data and on 100,000 movie ratings, with notes on the theory. Part of my research assistant work with Omiros Papaspiliopoulos at Bocconi.
- [**Rubik's cube solvers**](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/rubiks-cube-solvers): five solvers written from scratch, from the beginner's method to the shortest possible solution and a network trained by reinforcement learning, compared on the same scrambled cubes.
- [**Telomere length and the limit of human lifespan**](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/telomere-longevity): why the range of telomere lengths compatible with life narrows with age, and what that says about an upper limit to human lifespan. With Andrea Procopio and Lorenzo Battaglia.
- [**Simulated annealing on random 3-SAT**](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/simulated-annealing-3sat): how close simulated annealing gets to the point where random 3-SAT formulas stop having solutions, and how that changes with formula size and annealing time.
- [**Football player valuation**](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/football-player-value): predicting players' market values when a few stars dominate the error. With Andrea Procopio, Filippo Strub, Andrea Pettenon and Paride Lauretti.
- [**Benchmarking image upscalers**](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/upscaler-structural-similarity): interpolation filters, open neural networks and online services, scored by their structural similarity to the original photo.
- [**Graph convolutional networks on citation networks**](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/gcn-citation-networks): classifying papers by topic from their words and citations, reproducing Kipf and Welling on Cora, CiteSeer and PubMed.
- Also: strategies for [Mastermind](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/mastermind-solvers) and [hangman](https://github.com/lorenzocaputi/hangman-solvers).
